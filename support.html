Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my quit date?

On the home screen, tap "Had a slip? It's okay to reset." at the bottom. This will record a relapse and offer you personalized support. You can then start a fresh quit session.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, a summary of your progress is also saved to your personal iCloud account. We never see or access your data.

Can I transfer my data to a new device?

Yes. You have two options: (1) Enable iCloud sync before switching devices, or (2) Use Settings → Export Data to save a JSON backup, then import it on your new device via Settings → Import Data.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app removes all local data. To also remove iCloud data, go to Settings → disable iCloud sync before deleting the app, or clear it manually in iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Why is there no free trial?

MindQuit - Quit Smoking is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions. We believe in transparent pricing — you pay once and get everything, forever. No paywalls, no hidden costs.

Does this app replace medical advice?

No. MindQuit - Quit Smoking is a self-help tool based on cognitive restructuring techniques. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have health concerns related to smoking, please consult your doctor.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow the instructions.

すべてのデータはデバイス上にのみ保存されます。設定でiCloud同期を有効にすると、進捗の要約がお客様個人のiCloudアカウントにも保存されます。当社がお客様のデータを閲覧・アクセスすることはありません。
